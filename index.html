How your pizza is made, from dough to your door

FORNO
NEROWood-fired pizza

1 of 7

The dough

Flour, water, salt and a little yeast, left to rest for 48 hours. The slow rise gives a light, airy crust that's easy on the stomach.

2 of 7

The sauce

Ripe tomatoes crushed by hand with sea salt and fresh basil. No sugar, nothing from a tin.

3 of 7

The cheese

Fresh mozzarella, torn by hand so it melts into soft pools instead of a rubbery sheet.

4 of 7

The toppings

Vegetables chopped every morning, meats from suppliers we know by name. Basil goes on last.

5 of 7

The fire

Into the wood-fired oven at 450°C. Ninety seconds later the crust is blistered and the middle is molten.

6 of 7

The box

Sliced and sealed within a minute of leaving the oven, in a vented box that keeps the crust crisp on the way to you.

7 of 7

Your door

Hot at your door in about 35–45 minutes. Pick your pizzas and send the order on WhatsApp.
